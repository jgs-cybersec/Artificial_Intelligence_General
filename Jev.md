### LLM
**Large language models (LLMs)** are advanced artificial intelligence systems trained on massive amounts of text to understand, summarize, translate, and generate human-like language.

Most modern LLMs use deep learning and a neural network design called the transformer, which processes entire sentences at once rather than word-by-word.

They **operate through mathematical probability, predicting the most likely next piece of text (token)** based on context.

### Tokens

In natural language processing, tokens are the basic building blocks of text that a large language model (LLM) reads and generates.

Instead of reading text word-by-word or letter-by-letter, an LLM breaks sentences down into these smaller mathematical units. As a general rule of thumb, 1 token is roughly equal to 4 characters or 0.75 words in English.

**API Cost Management**: If you develop software using AI APIs (like OpenAI or Anthropic), you are billed strictly by the number of tokens processed (input) and tokens generated (output), rather than by word count.
